## 3. Fuel accounting, and the premises measured before use

- THE LOOP'S FUEL ARMS, measured against the generated
  `drive_nonmemory_steps_aux2_lemFuel` (Driver.lean:346-348): fuel `0` is
  the kill (`loop_zero_exhausts`, from the semantics' `_zero` lemma); at a
  PROGRAM-DONE configuration fuel exactly `1` records the step and the
  drain iteration on `[]` has no fuel — the kill (`loop_step_done_exhaust`,
  new); fuel `≥ 2` is PROGRAM-DONE (`loop_step_done`). So the total lane's
  `k + 2 ≤ fl` is EXACT for the drain, not slack (audit handoff R-3, §6).
- `driver2_lemFuel`'s `fuel` parameter (Driver.lean:381-384) is consumed by
  ONE outer round and passed only to the recursive `driver2_lemFuel lemFuel`
  inside `process_core_step2` (Driver.lean:384); the per-thread loop is
  called by `new_drive_core_threads` (Driver.lean:355-357) through its
  WRAPPER — verbatim `((drive_nonmemory_steps_aux2 _lemReader_tagDefs)
  fmapEmpty  /- NEXT -/ tids)`, i.e. at the fixed `10^8`. CONSEQUENCE
  (a falsification of the review §6's implied reading, recorded): the
  `∀ fuel` of the closed statement over `CerbND.drive_lemFuel fuel` bounds
  the OUTER `driver2` rounds only; at every `fuel ≥ 1` the statement is the
  one about the shipped `drive` (one `driver2` round is all a fragment run
  takes: the whole run happens inside the loop, `driver2_done`), and at
  `fuel = 0` the setup runs and `driver2_lemFuel 0` kills. The
  "partial correctness at every run length" content is the loop-level
  `DriverSafeCtl` (∀ `fl`); the shipped run is its instance at `fl = 10^8`.
  The design review §7 recorded a second upstream mirror fuelling both

  tree): measured by `#print axioms` to depend on NO axioms — it cannot sit
