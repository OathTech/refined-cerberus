## 2. The value-currency design ([AGENT])

**(A) Loaded values ride the existing currency.** The engine's `value`
already has `Vloaded (LVspecified ov | LVunspecified ty)` and `Vtuple`;
`SpikeVal`/`SpikeValA` carry any `value`. E2 therefore adds NO new
value type: it teaches the covered grammar `PePure` and the mirror
evaluator `evalPexpr` the constructors that BUILD loaded values
(`Cspecified`/`Cunspecified`/`Ctuple`, beside E1's `Civalignof`/
`Civsizeof`) and the form that TAKES THEM APART (`PEcase` at tuple and
`Specified` patterns). The evaluator is exact against the engine's arms
(`evalCtor_*`, `evalPexpr_case` — `select_case` computed by `rfl` at
authored patterns, as `specSum_select` in exhibit B). The `Specified`-
binder rules (`wps_seq_spec`/`wpt_seq_spec`) and the store/load rules
are untouched: they were stated at `Vloaded` values already.

**(B) `undef(UB…)` is a KILL arm, never a default.** The mirror
evaluator answers `none` at `PEundef` (the value does not exist), and
the OUTCOME is classified: `EvalFail := kill err | undef loc ubs` is
the one currency of the two failing faces; `EvalFail.run`/`EvalFail.pure`
is what the engine's state-threaded exception monad delivers (a raise —
state irrelevant; an undef — state untouched), and `EvalFail.reason` is
the shipped driver's kill (`Other (DErr_core_run err)` / `Undef0 loc ubs`,
`liftCore_run`). Every `*_kill` lemma of the E1 classifier became a
`*_fail` lemma stated at `EvalFail` with the `_kill` version a
corollary, and every `complete_*` row carries `ShippedRefusal.killed
fl.reason`. The design rule (brief, verbatim intent): "`undef(UB…)` arms
as fail-closed KILL (a `ShippedRefusal`/kill arm, never a default)".

**(C) The classifier mirrors the engine's PASSES.** `eval_pexpr_aux2`
iterates {pull, `step_eval_pexpr`, value test} until a value; `case`
returns the selected branch UNEVALUATED, so a branch's failure surfaces
in a LATER pass. The E1 classifier (one recursive `evalClass`) could not
say WHICH pass fails; E2's is structured as the engine is:

- `stepPexprRaw`/`stepPexpr` (Step.lean): ONE mirror pass — exactly
  `step_eval_pexpr`'s success shape on `PePure` (`stepPexpr_of_PePure`,
  `stepPexprRaw_eval_joint`, the `annot0`/`peStrip` laws);
- `stepFail`/`stepFailList` (EvalClass.lean, a WF mutual pair over
  `peDepth`): WHY the pass does not deliver — `StepFail := kill | undef
  | uncovered`, with equations `stepFail_sym`/`_val`/`_op`/`_array_shift`/
  `_ctor`/`_case`/`_not`/`_if`/`_undef`;
- `stepClass`: the pass's `StepOut := next r | kill | undef | uncovered`;
- `classIter`: the engine's iteration, terminating by the mirror's own
  depth guard (`isPePure r && peDepth r < peDepth pe`; beyond it
  `.uncovered`);
- `evalClass := match evalPexpr … with | some v => .val v | none =>
  classIter (peDepth pe) pe` — its `.val` face IS the mirror evaluator
  (`evalClass_val_iff`, statement verbatim E1's).

Operand LISTS in the engine's two fold shapes: `evalClassList`
(`stExceptUndef_mapM` — Ememop/Esave/Eproc operands: undefs COLLECTED,
the first EXCEPTION raised, `stExceptUndef_mapM_class_fail`,
`exception_undef_mapM_bridge`) and `evalClassFold` (`stExceptUndef_foldM`
— Erun arguments: the first failure, `foldM_args_fail`). The bridges are
proved level by level in the SUCCESS bridge's shape: `stepFail_bridge`,
`stepClass_bridge_fail`, `classIter_bridge`, `aux2_bridge_fail`,
`full_eval_bridge_fail`/`_undef`, `eval1_bridge_fail`, `mapM_eval1_fail`,
`mapM_full_eval_fail`, `mapM_save_fail`, `foldM_args_fail`; the
`_kill`-named E1 statements that survive are corollaries.

**(D) The `Unspecified` store.** No new rule (§1). The exhibit proves
the cell's post-image is the engine's byte image of `MVunspecified int`
— `fourBytes`/`unspecMval` in `EmittedBExhibit.lean`; `unspec_storable :
StorableAt tds intTy unspecMval` by `rfl`.

**(E) The tuple pattern is FLAT.** `TupleLeaf := List annot × Option sym
× core_base_type`; `tuplePat pa ls = Pattern pa (CaseCtor Ctuple (ls.map
leafPat))`. The corpus's binders are flat (t1: `(a_510: loaded integer,
a_511: loaded integer)`); nested tuple patterns at a binder are outside
`Frag` (the engine's `update_env` would zip them too — a mechanical
extension when a corpus program needs it).
