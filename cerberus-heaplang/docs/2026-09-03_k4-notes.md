## 3. THE MALLOC'D LIST: STOPPED AND REPORTED — the region-store gap (the arc's finding)

The charter's second exhibit was a malloc'd LINKED list: `alloc` `n`
region nodes, LINK them by stores through region views, walk and `free`
each. Decision, per the brief's three options, after measuring the tree:

- Is there a store rule over `regionOwn`? NO. `grep regionOwn Rules.lean
  Wps.lean Wpt.lean` hits only `alloc_atomic`/`free_atomic` and their
  faces. Every typed access rule — `store_atomic`, `load_atomic`,
  `storeAt_atomic`, `loadAt_atomic`, `load_atomic_readonly`, and the
  `wps`/`wpt` faces `wps_store`/`wps_load`/`wps_store_at`/`wps_load_at`/
  `wps_store_cell_at`/`wps_load_cell_at` — is stated over the OBJECT
  bundles (`pointsToCell`, `cellOwn`, `pointsToView`, `readonlyCell`:
  metadata `ty := some ty`, `dynamic := false`).
- Option (a), a `regionOwn ↔ typed-view` coercion law: UNSUPPORTED BY
  THE COUPLING, by design. `MetaCoh` (K1) pins a live cell's `ty` and
  `dynamic` to the engine's `Allocation` record (`LiveCoh.alloc : al.ty =
  mc.ty`, `MetaCoh.dynamic`), and `allocateRegion` records `ty := none`,
  pushing the base onto `dynamicAddrs`; `objCell` has `ty := some ty`,
  `dynamic := false`. A ghost update changing `ty` or `dynamic` would
  break the coupling — not a law, a falsehood. Not attempted.
- Option (b), "state the malloc'd-list exhibit at the byte level": there
  is no byte-level store rule either (the byte carrier `bytesOwn` is
  internal; no `AtomicStep` writes through it outside the typed rules),
  and without ANY store through a region the nodes cannot be linked in
  memory. Not available.
- Option (c), STOP AND REPORT THE GAP AS A FINDING: taken. The finding:
  K3 shipped `alloc`/`free` over `regionOwn` with no load/store rule over
  `regionOwn`/`regionView`, so a program that writes into malloc'd
  storage is outside the logic. The remedy is a RULE ADDITION, not a
  coupling change: the memM seams `loadM_live`/`storeM_live` (Heap.lean;
  K1) are stated at ANY metadata cell — `mc.ty` optional, the in-bounds
  check against `mc.size`, writability `mc.readonly = false` — so a
  `regionView`-shaped `storeAt_atomic`/`loadAt_atomic` twin (metadata
  `regionCell a n true` at `dqm`, bytes at `dqb`, the same `StorableView`
  premises, the decode fact at the accessed type) is provable with the
  existing seams, then two faces each, then manifest rows (`Frag.load`/
  `Frag.store` gain a second covering rule, as `Frag.kill` did). Sized:
  the create-vs-alloc analogy (K3 §4(f): one proof each, the object
  proof with the region bundle substituted). Recorded on every surface
  (README "Scope, exactly" (iv); ARCHITECTURE §7 open items; WALKTHROUGH
  §4/§7; Heap.lean `loadM_live`'s docstring). Nothing weakened: no rule
  restated, no premise dropped.

What was landed in its place — the region LOOP (§2) — is the budget's
first real loop client (the design note's "splittable capacity" now
carries a loop invariant, and the professor's "capacity is not a
∗-resource" residual has a consumer, not just a law), and it consumes
`wps_alloc`/`wpt_alloc`, `wps_free_emp`/`wpt_free_emp`,
`wps_kill_eval`/`wpt_kill_eval` at `Dynamic0` and `allocBudget_split` in
