## 1. Constructs admitted, and the engine as mirrored (cites: `frontend/model/core_reduction.lem`, `driver.lem` at the pin)

**`Eunseq es` — the unsequenced node.** Three engine facts, each
measured before the mirror was written and each a theorem of the
package now:

1. *Which component steps.* `get_ctx`'s `Eunseq` arm
   (core_reduction.lem:544–548): if every component `is_irreducible`, the
   node itself is the redex (`[(CTX, expr)]`); otherwise
   `get_ctx_unseq_aux annot [] [] es` (:590–601) walks the components
   left to right, skipping irreducible ones, and for each REDUCIBLE
   component PREPENDS its contexts (`zs ++ acc`, wrapped in `Cunseq annot
   es1 ctx es2`). So the engine's step list at a reducible `unseq` has one
   entry per reducible component, in LAST-FIRST order, and its head is
   the LAST reducible component's context. The shipped sequential loop
   (`drive_nonmemory_steps_aux2`, driver.lem:1063–1080) reads that list
   through `find_can_advance` (:1049–1058) — the FIRST advanceable entry
   — and every entry under a `Cunseq` frame is advanceable when the frame
   carries no `ccall` (fact 3). Mirror: `Step.unseq_ctx` steps the LAST
   reducible component (`valsOnly es2 = true` says every later component
   is a value); `Decomp.unseq` builds the `Cunseq` decomposition;
   `Decomp.get_ctx_at` is stated in HEAD form (§3); `apply_ctx_unseq` is
   the `Cunseq` arm of `apply_ctx` (:610–611).
2. *Completion.* `one_step0`'s `Eunseq` arm (:375–386) runs
   `one_step_unseq_aux ([], []) es` (:258–275): a `pure(v)` component
   contributes `v`; an `{A}pure(v)` component contributes `v` and
   COMBINES `A` into the accumulated footprints unless `do_race A acc`
   (:214–242) — a race is `Nothing` → `UNSEQUENCED_RACE` → the driver's
   `Step_with_runstate2 (RSK_eval "unsequenced race") (stExceptUndef_undef
   loc [UB035_unsequenced_race])`; otherwise `TAU "Eunseq"` into `Expr
   annots (Eannot fps (mk_value_e (Vtuple cvals)))`. Mirror:
   `collectUnseq` (Step.lean) is `one_step_unseq_aux` constructor for
   constructor over `SpikeValA` (`one_step_unseq_aux_collect` is the
   bridge, proved at every fuel); `Step.unseq_vals` is the TAU;
   `complete_unseq_vals` classifies the race as `ShippedRefusal.killed
   (Undef0 (locUpdTh an …).current_loc [UB035_unsequenced_race])`
   (`step_ctx_unseq_race`).
3. *Advanceability under the frame.* `is_unseq_with_ccall` (:501–519)
   walks the context; at a `Cunseq _ es1 ctx' es2` frame it ORs `List.any
   has_ccall (es1 ++ es2)` into the accumulator, and `Cbound` RESETS it to
   `false`. Requests whose context is "unseq with ccall" are not
   advanceable by the sequential driver. Mirror: `ccallFreeList (es1 ++
   es2) = true` is the sibling premise of `Step.unseq_ctx`/`Decomp.unseq`;
   `Decomp.unseq_ccall_false` proves `is_unseq_with_ccall ctx = false`
   for every fragment decomposition, through the bridges
   `ccallFree_has_ccall`/`ccallFreeList_has_ccall` to the engine's
   FUELLED `has_ccall_lemFuel` (`has_ccall_lemFuel_succ_*`).

**Determinism ([AGENT]).** The sequential driver is deterministic at an
`unseq`: `find_can_advance` returns the HEAD of `step_ctx`'s list
whenever the head is advanceable, and under a ccall-free `Cunseq` frame
every entry is (fact 3), so the step taken is the last reducible
component's — exactly `Step.unseq_ctx`'s choice; the singleton-execution
closed forms (`prod_run_eqJ_lib1`) therefore survive `unseq` unchanged.
The earlier reducible components' contexts (the list's tail) are never
read by the sequential loop; they matter only to the concurrency
driver, which is outside every statement here.

**The annotated-tuple weak binder.** The corpus's `let weak (a_510,
a_511) = unseq(…) in …` binds the `unseq`'s ANNOTATED tuple — E2's
`Step.wseq_tuple_annot` (LETW-ANNOT at the tuple binder), mirrored and
classified since E2, gains its rule face (§4, `wps_wseq_tuple_annot`).

**What is NOT admitted:** an `unseq` with a `ccall` component (the
frame is "unseq with ccall" — the sequential driver does not advance
its requests; E6's question), a `case`/`let`/`nd` component (§5, the
coarser `ccallFree`), an empty `unseq` (the printer's `BUG`), a `run` or
`pcall` inside an `unseq` (mirrored — `Step.unseq_inv`'s run/call
disjuncts — but ruled by no face; manifest NO-RULE).

## 2. The mirror design ([AGENT])

- **Focus = the last reducible component**, stated as `es1 ++ e :: es2`
  with `valsOnly es2 = true` (`focus_exists`/`focus_unique`: every
  non-`valsOnly` list has exactly one such split). `isValE` is the
  mirror's `is_irreducible` on the value protocol
  (`is_irreducible_eq_isValE`, `all_irreducible_eq_valsOnly`).
- **The spine searches descend through `Cunseq`** (design (A)):
  `jumpRedex?`/`callRedex?`/`redexAnnots`/`operandsOf` gain `U`-twins
  over the component list (`jumpRedexU?`, `callRedexU?`, `redexAnnotsU`,
  `operandsOfU`) that look into the focused component — faithful to the
  engine, needed by E6/E7 (a `run` or a call inside an `unseq` is then a
  mirror step today, `Step.unseq_inv`). `callRedex?_none_of_jumpRedex?_some`
  is a mutual theorem now.
- **`collectUnseq` in the premise** of `Step.unseq_vals` rather than the
  engine's `one_step_unseq_aux` (fuelled, over `generic_expr`): the
  mirror's premise is over `SpikeValA` and the bridge
  `one_step_unseq_aux_collect` is a theorem — the same pattern as E2's
  `update_env` and E3's `evalConvInt`.
- **`esize`/`pot` extended** by mutual `esizeList`/`potList` (`esize
  (Eunseq es) = 1 + esizeList es`, `pot (Eunseq es) = 1 + potList es`);
  `pot_step_bound`/`esize_le_pot`/`pot_le_two` carry the new cases.
- **`Frag.unseq (hne : es ≠ []) (hcc : ccallFreeList es = true) (∀ e ∈ es,
  Frag e)`**; `Frag.decomp`/`Frag.step` descend; `Step.ccallFree_preserved`
  (a component's step keeps it ccall-free) closes the frame's premise
  across rounds.

## 3. The head-form certification

`CerberusRound`, every arm of `ShippedRefusal`, both arms of
`OpenRound`, every `step_ctx_*`/`stepDischarge_*`/`loop_step_*` equation
and `Decomp.get_ctx_at`/`get_ctx_default`/`get_ctx_rebuild_action` are
restated from "the engine's step list is `[s]`" to "the engine's step
list is `s :: post`" (`∃ post`, or a `(post : List core_step2)` binder):

```lean
def CerberusRound (M : MachineCtx) (c c' : Config) : Prop :=
  ∀ dst : driver_state, M.Embeds dst c →
    ∃ (s : core_step2) (post : List core_step2),
      step_ctx M.tagDefs dst.layout_state dst.core_file dst.core_extern M.tid
        (M.parent, M.thread c.1 c.2.1 c.2.2.1) = s :: post ∧
      can_advance s = true ∧
      …
```

This is the MINIMAL generalisation the engine forces (§1 fact 1): `pre =
[]` always, because the focused component is the last reducible one and
its contexts are prepended last. The design's wording (`∃ pre post,
step_ctx … = pre ++ s :: post`, design §C.4:763–765) is stronger than
needed; recorded as a deviation (§5). The singleton reading is a theorem
where it held before: `step_ctx_length` (the list's length is the number
of contexts), `step_ctx_singleton_of_root` (a root redex: `[s]`),
`Decomp.get_ctx_single` (a `ctxNoUnseq` decomposition: `[s]`) — so
`overflow_step_ctx`, the `engine_complete_*U` witnesses and every pre-E4
exhibit statement are textually unchanged. `frag_round_complete`,
`cerberusRound_classify`, `step_iff_cerberusRound`: statements textually
unchanged (the definitions under them changed shape). Proof technique
for the ∃-forms: `have key : (step_ctx …).head? = some X ∧ …` then
`cons_of_head?` — the direct `refine ⟨_, _, List.cons_eq_cons.mpr …⟩`
left synthetic-opaque metavariables and `simp` timed out on the residual
`List.map F rest` of the step_ctx lambda.

```lean
      wpt M p Ls Θ k Ψ (Expr a (Eunseq (ws.map ofValA))) ρ

theorem wpt_wseq_tuple_annot {Ψ : SpikeVal → EnvStack → IProp GF}
    (a pa : List annot) (ls : List TupleLeaf)
    (e1 e2 : CoreExpr)
    (ev0 : Fmap sym value) (evs : List (Fmap sym value)) (k1 k2 : Nat) :
    wpt M p Ls Θ k1 (fun w ρ' => iprop(∃ (vs : List value) (ds : List dyn_annotation),
        ⌜w = SpikeVal.annot ds (Vtuple vs)⌝ ∗
        wpt M p Ls Θ k2 Ψ (Expr [] (Eannot ds e2)) (update_env (tuplePat pa ls) (Vtuple vs) ρ')))

      wpt M p Ls Θ (k1 + k2) Ψ (Expr a (Ewseq (tuplePat pa ls) e1 e2))
        (ev0 :: evs)
```

Reynolds/O'Hearn reading: `wps_unseq_focus` is a bind/frame rule for
the `Cunseq` evaluation context — the focused component is verified on
its own (its heap resources are its own; the siblings are inert values
and consume nothing), and the continuation receives the delivered value
in ANY annotation shape (`∀ wa, ⌜wa.erase = w⌝ -∗ …`) because the
component's dynamic annotations are what the completion combines;
`wps_unseq_vals` is the completion — the annotated tuple `{fps}(cvals)`
with `collectUnseq` computing both. The total faces add the budget:
`k1 + k2` (the component's, then the rest's) and `3 ≤ k` for the
completion (one TAU round, delivery cost 2 for the annotated value).
Companions: `fupd_wps`, `fupd_wpt_nonval` (a fancy update in front of a
judgment at a non-value — the proof device the focus rules' `iloeb`
needs), `wpt_jump_frame_unseq` (the jump frame under `Cunseq`, twin of
`wpt_jump_frame_bound`).

**`wps_pure`/`wpt_pure` generalised** from `Expr [] (Epure pe)` to `Expr a
(Epure pe)` (text change: `{a : List annot}` implicit; the E2 statement
is the instance `a := []`). Forced: t1's `pure(x)` under the weak binder
and its `pure(case …)` under `bound` carry `Aloc` annotations
(`t1.annot.core`), and the delivered value is `.pure v` at any `a`
(`wps_ofValA (.pure a [] v)`, `SpikeValA.erase_pure`).

## 5. Deviations and decision points ([AGENT])

1. **`s :: post`, not `pre ++ s :: post`** (design §C.4): the engine's
   list at a reducible `unseq` is last-reducible-first, so the focused
   component's contexts HEAD the list — `pre = []` is a theorem, not a
   choice. Recorded; the design's stronger shape would have carried an
   inert `pre` through 130 statements.
2. **`ccallFree` excludes `Ecase`/`Elet`/`End` components as well as
   `Eccall`.** The engine's `has_ccall` (the `is_unseq_with_ccall` leaf)
   walks INTO `case` branches, `let` bodies and `nd` alternatives; the
   mirror's sibling condition is the coarser syntactic one — a `case`
   component inside an `unseq` is outside `Frag` (fail-closed: never
   mis-stepped, `uncoveredKinds` reports `unseq-component-ccall-or-case`).
   The corpus places no such component inside an `unseq`; E5/E6 revisit
   if the emitted `if`/`switch` lands inside one.
3. **`collectUnseq` (a mirror function over `SpikeValA`) in the
   premise** instead of the engine's `one_step_unseq_aux` — bridged by
   `one_step_unseq_aux_collect` at every fuel (the engine's function is
   fuelled by the lem→Lean scheme).
4. **Design (A) for the spine searches** (descend through `Cunseq`) over
   the cheaper (B) (stop at `Eunseq`): (B) would have made a `run` inside
   an `unseq` a non-step (outside `Frag`) and E7's `t9` needs the jump
   under the frame.
5. **`Frag.unseq` requires `es ≠ []`**: the printer marks an empty
   `unseq` as `BUG` (pp_core.ml:639–642) and `collectUnseq ([], []) [] =
   some ([], [])` would otherwise deliver `{}()` — a shape the elaborator
   never emits.
6. **Pins: exhaustive.** Every new theorem measured trio-exact is pinned
   (62), including the exhibit's frame-lookup helpers — E3 left its
   `frXC_*` helpers unpinned; the exhaustive convention is the
   fail-closed one (a pin is a check, not a claim). Sub-trio facts are
   listed unpinned in `Audit.lean`'s E4 paragraph (§8).
7. **`wpt_unseq_vals` at `3 ≤ k`** (not `2 ≤ k`): the completion TAU
   round costs 1 and the annotated value's delivery 2 (`deliveryCost`).
8. **The skeleton reads `save` initialisers** (E4 brief (iii)) by a
   dedicated tokenizer path (`saveInits`/`cutInit`/`skipTypeToAssign`)
   rather than by admitting `:=` into `pexScan` (which would have
   accepted `:=` anywhere in a pure expression — over-permissive).
9. **No partial-lane engine statement for t1** (`t1_engine`): the brief's
   milestone is the closed shipped-driver statement; `t1_wps` is proved
   (the partial derivation) and `t1_blockSpecs` with it, but no
   `engine_adequacy` instance was assembled — `t1_blockSpecs` is
   therefore consumerless (for the orchestrator's KOI, §10 H-1).
10. **H-1 (optional hygiene) not done**: the triplicated `depLe*`, the
    `symC_eval`/`symK_eval` twins and the labels-proof copies stand; the
    T1 exhibit adds its own frame boilerplate in the same pattern.
