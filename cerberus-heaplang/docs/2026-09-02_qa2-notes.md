`wp_annot_reindex`/`wp_annot`/`wp_sseq` are FALSE at a populated label

the premise's continuation and the conclusion's land on the same
registered body owing DIFFERENT postconditions. The classical raw-WP
rule `triple P e1 Q → (∀ v, triple (Q v) e2 R) → triple P (e1; e2) R`
