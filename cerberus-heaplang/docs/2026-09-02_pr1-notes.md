## 6. Required fix 8 — one proof per small axiom (commit B)

**Finding (Q1/§3.8).** Each small axiom proved three times by the same
~80-line unfolding against `Step`.

**Why not the suggested `wps_of_wp_atomic`.** iris-lean's `wp.pre`
places its `▷` AFTER the step's update (`£ (numLatersPerStep ns + 1)
={∅}▷=∗^[…] |={∅,E}=> …`, WeakestPre.lean:73-83), while `wps.pre`'s step
clause — deliberately the `wp_lift_step` premise shape — places `▷`
BEFORE the `∀` over steps. Deriving the latter from the former needs
`|={∅}=> ▷ P ⊢ ▷ |={∅}=> P`, which is not a law of the logic (the
frame-preserving update at step `n` does not restrict to one at `n-1`
in the direction needed), and the clause's single later credit is
spent feeding the raw WP. The other direction (`wps` at `Ls := False`
⊢ `WP`) exists (`wps_sound`) but only at mask `⊤`, so `wp_store`'s
mask-generic statement cannot be a corollary of `wps_store` either.

**What was done.** The common ancestor: the mask-generic ATOMIC STEP
SPECIFICATION (Rules.lean)
