The `Language` instance (Lang.lean): `primStep p _ q := Step p.1.M
(p.1.e, p.1.ρ, p.1.ctl, p.2) (q.1.e, q.1.ρ, q.1.ctl, q.2.1) ∧ q.1.M =
p.1.M ∧ q.2.2 = []` — the control is NOT pinned by `primStep` (C2's
call/return rules write it); `toVal := toValRt`, so a value at a
non-empty stack is NOT a Language value (the design note's point: it is
the RETURN redex). The partial-bijection laws forced `CoreRVal`'s shape:
`toVal_coe : toVal (ofVal v) = some v` must hold for EVERY `v`, so a
value cannot carry a `κ` (an `ofVal` at `κ ≠ []` would be a non-value);
it carries the terminal control's `proc`/`execLoc` and `ofValRt` puts it
at `κ = []`. Two new lemmas do the C1 bookkeeping: `Step.ctl_eq` (no
rule of this slice writes the control; `induction h <;> first | rfl |
assumption`) and `Step.retag` (the same fact on the step, for the
congruence rules fed by Language-interface successors).

## 3. The canonical embedding (the "old = new" argument)

Write `M₀` for an old context. Its embedding is the pair
`embed M₀ := (⌊M₀⌋, ⟨[], M₀.proc, M₀.execLoc⟩)` with `⌊M₀⌋` the new
context dropping the three fields. Under `SeqWF M₀` (old:
`M₀.stack = Stack_empty ∧ M₀.parent = none`):
- `⌊M₀⌋.thread e ρ ⟨[], M₀.proc, M₀.execLoc⟩ = M₀.thread e ρ` — `rfl`
  after `M₀.stack = Stack_empty` (`Ctl.toStack_nil`);
- `⌊M₀⌋.labelsAt ⟨[], M₀.proc, _⟩.proc = M₀.labels` — `rfl` (the same
  two-level lookup at the same symbol);
- the new hypothesis `hκ : ctl.κ = []` is `rfl`; `SeqWF ⌊M₀⌋` is
  `⟨hwf.parent⟩`;
- every `Step M₀ (e, ρ, σ) (e', ρ', σ')` is `Step ⌊M₀⌋ (e, ρ, c₀, σ)
  (e', ρ', c₀, σ')` at `c₀ := ⟨[], M₀.proc, M₀.execLoc⟩` and back
  (every constructor is the same constructor with the control
  threaded; `Step.ctl_eq` shows a new step cannot leave `c₀`);
- `MemTripleU M₀ ρ e P ψ` (old) is `MemTripleU ⌊M₀⌋ c₀ ρ e P ψ` (new),
  definitionally, and likewise `SemTripleU`, `ProvenTripleU`,
  `MemTripleU_alloc`, `DriveDoneAt`, `StateInertLabels`, `outcomesU`,
  `engineStepsU`, `CerberusRound`, `MachineCtx.Embeds`,
  `ShippedRefusal`, `OpenRound`, `RoundClass`, `EngineMatchU`;
- the judgments: `wps M₀ Ls Ψ e ρ` (old) is `wps ⌊M₀⌋ c₀ Ls Ψ e ρ` —
  `wps.pre` reads the label map at `ctl.proc` and the tuple at `ctl`,
  which at `c₀` are the old reads; the same for `wpt`, `blockSpecs`,
  `blockSpecsT`, `AtomicStep`.
So every changed statement of §4, instantiated at `embed M₀`, is the
old statement (and every export the exhibits consume is consumed at an
entry control — `spikeCtl`, `procCtl p` — whose `κ` is `[]` by `rfl`).

DESIGN DECISIONS taken in the slice ([AGENT], each named with its
forcing fact; none touches the trust surface):
1. The judgments `wps`/`wpt`/`blockSpecs`/`blockSpecsT`/`AtomicStep`
   are indexed by the FULL control `ctl` (not only by `proc`): the
   step clause needs the whole tuple `⟨e, ρ, ctl, M⟩`, and this keeps
   every rule's text unchanged except for the index (`wps M Ls …` →
   `wps M ctl Ls …`). C3's procedure-indexed `wps M f Ls Π …` then
   quantifies the control in the CPS collapse, as the design note
   prescribes; the index is a strict generalization of C1's.
2. `hκ : ctl.κ = []` is an explicit hypothesis of the collapses
   (`wps_sound`, `wps_sound_frame`, `wpt_sound`), of the raw-WP lifting
   (`wp_of_atomic`, `wp_store`, `wp_load`) and of every adequacy export
   at a general control — rather than fixing `ctl := ⟨[], p, ℓ⟩` in
   the statements — so that C3's CPS collapse replaces it by the
   return clause without restating the exports' shape.
3. `MachineCtx.labels` became `labelsAt (p : Option sym)` (the read is
   at the LIVE procedure); `procCtx p rs` became `procCtx rs` with the
   entry control `procCtl p` (the procedure has nowhere else to live);
   `Config` moved from Round.lean to Step.lean (Step is stated over it).
4. `SeqWF` shrank to `parent = none`; "empty stack, in procedure `p`"
   are entry facts of the launches (`spikeCtl_κ`, `procCtl_κ`,
   `procCtl_proc`, all `rfl`).
